> A skill that diagnoses common OpenClaw errors and suggests fixes automatically

I don't have access to the previous conversation context. To continue where you left off, I need to know what you were working on.

Could you please tell me:

1. What task you were performing
2. What you were trying to accomplish
3. Where you got stuck or what failed

Once I understand the context, I can help you continue from where you left off.
